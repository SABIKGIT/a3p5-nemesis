# A3P5 NEMESIS research package

## What this package establishes
The photographed prototype is the reference for the geometric model. The manuscript is a design and analytical study, not a report of validated integrated rover performance. Mass, centre of mass, battery capacity and several operating parameters remain assumed. Public-dataset calibration results are not NEMESIS sensor accuracy.

## Start here
- `final/`: editable Word manuscript and Markdown source; the complete LaTeX project is in `latex/`; rendered equation images accompany the build assets.
- `research/RESULTS.md`: exact exploratory metadata-census method and final counts.
- `research/references_verified.json`: selected, verified references with source IDs and examination depth.
- `analysis/analysis_results.json`: declared mechanical/electrical assumptions and computed results.
- `analysis/ml_outputs/results.json`: actual external-data benchmark results and cleaning audit.
- `blender/A3P5_Nemesis_Mission_Studies.blend`: editable rover and mission scenes. Principal component positions and managed wiring are preserved; added patrol sensors are explicitly proposed.
- `diagrams/`: complete native Canva PDF, print-ready page images, editable-design provenance and source specifications.

## Literature scope
5,000 metadata occurrences were retrieved in ten Crossref queries. DOI deduplication produced 4,897 distinct records, of which 1,291 had abstracts. Screening v1.2 prioritized 1,212 candidates and created a 150-record reading shortlist. This is an exploratory metadata census, not 5,000 full-text reviews or a systematic review. The selected-source registry separately documents actual examination depth. The full registry is broader than the manuscript's cited-reference list.

## Reproduce numerical analysis
Use Python 3.12 with the exact package versions in `reproducibility_versions.json` (also `requirements-analysis.txt`). From the manuscript directory:

`python analysis/engineering_analysis.py`

`python analysis/ml_benchmark.py`

The scripts resolve inputs relative to their own locations. They can import optional dependencies from `python_packages/`, if present, or from the active Python environment. The large local dependency/runtime directories are not distributed in the research archive. Exact code, input data, split assignments, predictions, model-selection records and numerical tables are included. Rerunning reproduces the numerical study; desktop timing may differ.

The UCI Air Quality archive is retained under `research/air_quality.zip` with its SHA-256 in the benchmark audit. Cite Vito (2008), UCI Machine Learning Repository, DOI 10.24432/C59K5F. The archived file contains 9,357 valid timestamps, differing from repository prose; use the documented file-derived count. Missing target observations and all-predictor-missing records are excluded, leaving 7,344 eligible observations. Models are fitted on the first 70%, chosen on the next 15%, and assessed on the untouched final 15%. The chosen Ridge model is retained even though a different model has a slightly smaller test error. Model files are generated by this package; exact versions are required for reliable replay.

## Geometry and rendering
Open the mission-study `.blend` in Blender 4.3.2 or a compatible newer version. Scenes M01–M06 contain industrial observation, water assessment, lightweight sample handling, reconnaissance, proposed patrol and an engineering view. M02 and M03 include detail cameras. Rendered mission pictures are conceptual design illustrations, not logged missions. The model's geometry is estimated from four distinct photographs, recovered byte-for-byte from the packed original reference images. The optional lift configuration is preserved in the original industrial-study scene.

All materials used for the main model are local/procedural. Carbon appearance does not establish a real carbon-composite lay-up or strength. Wiring is secured in the shown pose; full articulation and fatigue require physical identification and testing. Analytical axes are x forward, y left, z up from the ground projection of chassis centre; Blender mapping is(X,Y,Z)=(y,-x,z).

## Canva diagrams
The fourteen active diagrams were authored as complete editable Canva pages. Each page contains its manuscript figure number, title, nodes, connectors, arrowheads and arrow legend. The user-supplied native Canva PDF is retained at `diagrams/canva_complete_native/A3P5_Canva_Final_Figures_Print.pdf`; its SHA-256 and per-page image hashes are recorded in `native_export_manifest.json` in the same folder. The print assets `diagrams/canva_01_print.png` through `canva_14_print.png` are whole-page rasterizations of that PDF, preserving the complete 1860 × 1200 canvas.

Final editable Canva design: https://www.canva.com/d/tCSQwYfshs9lo2F

`diagrams/canva_complete_final_provenance.json` identifies the final design and its page-to-figure mapping. The accompanying complete-page HTML, generator, semantic specifications and route definitions document the authoring source; the final Canva design and supplied export are authoritative for publication. Regenerating the local source alone does not replace native Canva export and visual verification. Historical component-tile assembly files are excluded from the active research archive.

The fixed canvas aspect ratio and manuscript image bounds preserve figure placement. To regenerate page images from the supplied PDF, rasterize its fourteen pages to 1860 × 1200 pixels and preserve the full canvas; then verify figure numbers, legends and per-page source hashes before replacing print assets. Any newly exported Canva revision requires fresh hashes and manuscript visual review.

## Manuscript layout
The requested reference is Reina and Foglia's 2010 paper, DOI 10.3182/20100906-3-IT-2019.00036. Its signed PDF link had expired and publisher/author-repository retrieval did not provide the full PDF. The manuscript therefore uses an honestly described single-column IFAC-inspired adaptation, not an exact inspected reproduction or publisher-endorsed template. Author affiliations, email addresses, contributions and funding are supplied by the user. Final submission declarations require author review.

## Research limits
No journal acceptance is implied. Claims of prototype sensor accuracy, payload capability, endurance, slope capability, autonomous navigation, environmental safety certification or completed field missions require the experiments described in the manuscript. The study provides reproducible expectations against which those experiments can be compared.

## Metadata distribution
The publication archive preserves bibliographic metadata, abstract-presence flags, query logs, hashes and screening outputs. It omits publisher abstract text and raw responses containing that text; raw retrieval files remain in the local research workspace. Re-running retrieval/screening requires obtaining the original metadata from Crossref. The exported hashes identify the original responses, while distributable tables preserve the reported screening decisions. Crossref notes that abstracts can retain publisher or author copyright: https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/.
