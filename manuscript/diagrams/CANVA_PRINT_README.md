## Authoring and verification

`A3P5_Canva_Figures_With_Legends.html`, `build_canva_legend_pages.py`, the two `canva_revision_spec_*.json` files and `canva_special_routes.json` retain complete-page authoring specifications. The final Canva design and exported PDF include the native publication content and remain authoritative. A local preview or source regeneration is not a new verified Canva export.

The current manuscript uses widths from 5.89 to 6.80 inches, with the 1.55 canvas aspect ratio preserved. Check legibility at each actual print size, including arrow direction, legend meaning, state-transition labels and complete node text. Any export revision requires fresh source/page hashes and manuscript review. The source layout audit reports local preview checks; final native-PDF and manuscript checks are recorded separately.

Historical component-tile designs and assembly records remain local process history. They are omitted from the active distribution, which contains the fourteen complete native pages and their current source/provenance records.
