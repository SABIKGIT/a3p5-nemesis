# Engineering calculations and evidence boundaries

This repository reproduces a design study. **The engineering outputs are conditional calculations, not measured rover performance.** The photographed prototype, Blender reconstruction, external-data benchmark and future hardware tests have different evidential roles.

| Evidence | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Supplied prototype photographs | Visible arrangement of the enclosure, wheels, arm, mast and sampling equipment | Dimensions, material strength, feedback instrumentation or operating limits |
| Blender reconstruction and mission views | Estimated geometry, component placement and cable routing in the displayed poses | Successful missions, full-travel cable clearance, load capacity or environmental protection |
| Engineering scripts and tables | Reproducible results for declared equations and inputs | An experimentally identified rover model |
| UCI Air Quality benchmark | Held-out prediction scores on an external historical sensor dataset | NEMESIS sensor accuracy, embedded timing or autonomous capability |
| Hardware test templates | Proposed measurements and acceptance-rule definitions | Executed tests or approved numerical thresholds |

The analytical frame is right handed: **x forward, y left, z upward**, with its origin at the ground-plane projection of the chassis centre on level support. The Blender conversion is `(X, Y, Z) = (y, -x, z)`. Positive stability slope is downhill toward the rover's front. Angles passed to trigonometric functions are radians; displayed plots and slope tables use degrees. Wheel torque below is **gearbox-output torque**, not motor-shaft torque.

## 1. Compatible wheel commands

For wheel position `(x_i, y_i)`, body translation `(v_x, v_y)` and yaw rate `omega`:

```text
u_i = v_x - omega*y_i
w_i = v_y + omega*x_i
delta_i = atan2(w_i, u_i)
Omega_i = sqrt(u_i² + w_i²) / r_i
wheel_rpm = 60*Omega_i/(2*pi)
```

The assumption is planar rigid-body rolling without slip. Equivalent steering and signed rolling commands `(delta_i + pi, -Omega_i)` describe the same wheel velocity; they do not establish that the real steering mechanism can reach either configuration. Near-zero wheel speed requires an explicit heading policy. Steering travel, rate, cable clearance, backlash and traction must be identified separately.

At 0.25 m/s and radius 0.140 m, ideal straight wheel speed is **17.052315 rpm**. This is an output-wheel requirement; gearing and encoders have not been specified by this calculation.

## 2. Grade demand, traction and energy

```text
F_req = m*a + m*g*sin(alpha) + C_rr*m*g*cos(alpha) + F_ext
tau_w = r*F_req/4
F_req <= mu*m*g*cos(alpha)
E_use_Wh = V_nom*C_Ah*f_DoD*f_E
P_battery_W = F_req*v/eta_d + P_aux
endurance_h = E_use_Wh/P_battery_W
```

The saved grade/endurance summary uses 24 kg, zero acceleration and external force, four equal longitudinal force shares, 0.25 m/s and 20 W auxiliaries. Estimated usable energy is **84.915 Wh**.

| Grade | Required force (N) | Required torque per wheel (N·m) | Minimum uniform friction coefficient | Battery demand (W) | Endurance (h) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0° | 14.121576 | 0.494255 | 0.060000 | 24.412992 | 3.478271 |
| 10° | 54.776803 | 1.917188 | 0.236327 | 37.117751 | 2.287719 |
| 20° | 93.767665 | 3.281868 | 0.423970 | 49.302395 | 1.722330 |
| 30° | 129.909444 | 4.546831 | 0.637350 | 60.596701 | 1.401314 |

The friction inequality is only a necessary screen under uniform loading/friction. Each real contact also has its own normal force and traction limit. Soft-soil deformation, steering scrub, wheel unloading, thermal limits and voltage sag are omitted. Do not compare stall torque with sustained demand or apply a gearbox ratio twice to an output-shaft rating. The 0.90 energy derating and 0.80 traction efficiency act on different quantities; auxiliary demand is not divided by traction efficiency. Constant-grade endurance is not a route-duration prediction.

## 3. Payload and geometric stability

```text
p_CG = (m_b*p_b + m_p*p_p)/(m_b + m_p)
M_front = L/2 - x_CG - h_CG*tan(beta)
beta_tip = atan((L/2 - x_CG)/h_CG)
```

`M_front` is a projected distance in metres, not a stability probability. Zero is the ideal geometric front-tipping boundary. The model holds base COM fixed while adding payload; it does not move the arm-link masses.

| Added payload (kg) | COM x (m) | COM z (m) | Front-tipping bound (degrees) | Front margin at 20° (m) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0.000000 | 0.580000 | 38.052369 | 0.242897 |
| 1 | 0.032000 | 0.596800 | 35.264305 | 0.204783 |
| 2 | 0.061538 | 0.612308 | 32.658035 | 0.169600 |

These are **not approved operating slopes or payloads**. Actual arm pose, external contact, acceleration, compliance, ground geometry and measurement uncertainty must enter a measured operating envelope. Do not use the zero-margin angle as a hardware test target.

## 4. Response lag and stopping-clearance scenarios

For a normalized first-order step, `response(t) = 1 - exp(-t/tau)`. The characteristic distance `v*tau` differs from the distance to 90% response:

```text
t90 = tau*ln(10)
d90 = v*tau*ln(10)
```

For `tau = 15 s` at `v = 0.25 m/s`, `v*tau = 3.75 m`, whereas **t90 = 34.538776 s** and **d90 = 8.634694 m**. Faster acquisition alone does not remove sensor dynamics. The scenario does not model chemical selectivity, heater transitions, humidity or asymmetric recovery.

The separate stopping model is `d_clear = v*T_latency + v²/(2*a_brake) + d_margin`. With the declared assumptions, clearance is **0.430 m at 0.4 m/s**, or **1.450 m at 1.0 m/s**. Available deceleration and detection/command delay require ground-level measurements. The fixed allowance does not confer fail-safe behaviour or certified emergency-stop performance; a power interruption can behave differently from commanded braking.

## 5. pH conditioning and ideal ADC resolution

```text
S(T) = ln(10)*R*T/F                 # T in kelvin
E = E_7 - S(T)*(pH - 7)
V_ADC = V_mid + G*S(T)*(7 - pH)     # declared sign/gain convention
LSB_V = V_ref/(2**N)
delta_pH_per_LSB = V_ref/(2**N*G*S(T))
```

At 25 °C, the ideal slope magnitude is **59.159350 mV/pH**. With gain 3, the 5 V/10-bit scenario has **4.8828125 mV/LSB** and **0.027512205 pH/LSB**; the proposed 3.3 V/12-bit scenario has **0.004539514 pH/LSB**. A 10-bit converter has 1,024 intervals and codes 0–1,023. Use `2**N` for ideal LSB width, not the maximum code.

These are ideal resolutions, not accuracy specifications. Electrode polarity, offset, slope, high-impedance buffering, leakage, reference voltage, noise and temperature require calibration. The saved table contains continuous ideal code coordinates, not simulated integer ADC readings. A bare glass electrode is not a demonstrated direct MCU input. The plotted positive gain and midpoint are assumed interface choices.


This regenerates calculated outputs; it does not run the rover. Use the repository's environment instructions and [recorded requirements](../manuscript/requirements-analysis.txt). These deterministic sweeps are not uncertainty intervals. Numerical audit records check calculations and saved data consistency, not hardware performance.

## External-data result kept separate

The [benchmark protocol](../manuscript/analysis/ml_outputs/benchmark_protocol.json) and [results](../manuscript/analysis/ml_outputs/results.json) record 7,344 eligible UCI observations, split chronologically into 5,140 training, 1,102 validation and 1,102 test rows. Preprocessing is fitted on training data; 21 candidates are selected by validation RMSE. There is no train-plus-validation refit or test-based selection.

Validation-selected Ridge (`alpha = 10`) has test CO RMSE **0.502 mg/m³**, with a conditional 95% daily-block bootstrap interval **0.435–0.569 mg/m³**. The resampling uses 2,000 paired draws of 48 observed test days. It preserves within-day clusters but does not capture fitted-model uncertainty or cross-day dependence. The archived CSV has 9,357 valid timestamps; documented counts follow the file rather than inconsistent repository summary prose. Dataset attribution: [Vito, UCI Air Quality, DOI 10.24432/C59K5F](https://doi.org/10.24432/C59K5F).

Use the [hardware validation plan](hardware-validation-plan.md) to replace declared assumptions with traceable rover measurements. Do not copy any calculated or UCI result into the blank hardware measurement fields.
