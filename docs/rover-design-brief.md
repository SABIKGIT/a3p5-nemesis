# A3P5 NEMESIS design brief

A3P5 NEMESIS is a photographed four-wheel rover used as the reference for an environmental inspection and lightweight sampling design study. Retain the independently steered wheel assemblies, box enclosure with a carbon-pattern finish, folded manipulator, inclined camera/antenna mast and side-mounted sampling equipment. There is no solar panel.

Industrial details include protected wiring, connector boots, cable clamps, sealed-looking enclosures, fasteners, motor housings and service interfaces. These are visual design interpretations. Cable curves are static; a secure appearance in one pose does not establish articulation clearance, fatigue life, electrical continuity or ingress protection.

Mission-study scenes illustrate industrial observation, water assessment, lightweight sample handling, reconnaissance, proposed patrol and an engineering overview. Scene renders are conceptual arrangements, not evidence that the physical rover completed these missions. Additional patrol sensors and hidden electronics are proposed equipment.

Geometry is estimated from four distinct reference photographs. Mass, battery capacity, centre of mass and several operating values are declared analytical assumptions. The companion calibration benchmark uses the public UCI Air Quality dataset, not measurements from the rover. Hardware validation protocols are provided separately.

This public brief summarizes the physical layout and documented study scope; it is not a fabrication drawing, wiring schematic or validated firmware specification.
